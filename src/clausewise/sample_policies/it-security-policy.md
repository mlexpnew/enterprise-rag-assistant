# IT and Information Security Policy

## 1. Accounts
1.1 Passwords must be at least 12 characters and are changed every 90 days.
1.2 Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, HRMS and all customer systems.

## 2. Devices
2.1 USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops.
2.2 Personal phones can access company email only after enrolling in the company MDM app.

## 3. Incidents
3.1 Suspected security incidents, phishing emails or lost laptops must be reported to security@nimbus.example within 1 hour.
3.2 Customer personal data must never be copied to personal devices or shared in public AI tools.
