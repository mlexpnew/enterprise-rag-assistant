# Compensation Bands (Confidential: HR only)

## 1. Salary bands
1.1 Annual CTC band for grade G3 is ₹9,00,000 to ₹14,00,000.
1.2 Annual CTC band for grade G5 is ₹22,00,000 to ₹32,00,000.

## 2. Increments
2.1 The FY 2025-26 increment budget is 9% of total payroll.
