# Appraisal Guidelines for Managers

## 1. Ratings
1.1 Rating distribution guideline: 20% exceeds expectations, 70% meets expectations and 10% below expectations.
1.2 Ratings are calibrated in a department review before they are shared with employees.

## 2. Performance improvement
2.1 A performance improvement plan (PIP) runs for 60 days, with fortnightly check-ins documented on HRMS.
