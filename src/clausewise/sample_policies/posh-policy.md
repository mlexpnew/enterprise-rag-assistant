# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Policy

## 1. Internal Committee
1.1 Nimbus Retail has an Internal Committee (IC) under the POSH Act, 2013, presided over by a senior woman employee and including one external member.
1.2 The IC can be reached at ic@nimbus.example.

## 2. Complaints
2.1 A complaint should be filed in writing within 3 months of the incident; the IC may extend this by a further 3 months for valid reasons.
2.2 The inquiry is completed within 90 days of receiving the complaint.
2.3 The identity of the complainant and witnesses is kept confidential.
