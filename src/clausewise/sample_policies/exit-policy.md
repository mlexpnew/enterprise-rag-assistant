# Resignation and Exit Policy

## 1. Notice period
1.1 The notice period is 30 days for grades G1 to G3, 60 days for grades G4 to G6 and 90 days for grade G7 and above.
1.2 Notice period buyout is allowed with the approval of the department head, at basic salary for the unserved days.

## 2. Full and final settlement
2.1 Full and final (F&F) settlement is paid within 45 days of the last working day.
2.2 Gratuity is paid to employees who have completed 5 years of continuous service, as per the Payment of Gratuity Act.
