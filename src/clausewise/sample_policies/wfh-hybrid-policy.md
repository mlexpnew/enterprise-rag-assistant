# Hybrid and Work From Home Policy

## 1. Eligibility
1.1 Employees who have completed their probation can work from home up to 2 days per week, with manager approval on the HRMS portal.
1.2 Store and warehouse roles are not eligible for work from home.

## 2. Working hours
2.1 Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST; employees must be reachable on Teams during core hours.

## 3. Allowances
3.1 Internet reimbursement: employees approved for hybrid work can claim up to ₹1,000 per month for home internet.
3.2 A one-time home office allowance of ₹5,000 is paid for a chair or desk, with an invoice.
