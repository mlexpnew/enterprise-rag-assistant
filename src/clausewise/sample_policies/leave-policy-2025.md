# Leave Policy (FY 2025-26)

## 1. Scope
1.1 This policy applies to all permanent employees of Nimbus Retail Pvt Ltd in India and replaces the Leave Policy v1.0.

## 2. Leave entitlement
2.1 Privilege leave (PL): employees earn 21 days of privilege leave per calendar year, credited monthly at 1.75 days.
2.2 Casual leave (CL): employees get 8 days of casual leave per year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.
2.3 Sick leave (SL): employees get 12 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for more than 2 consecutive days.
2.4 Carry forward: up to 45 days of unused privilege leave can be carried forward to the next year.
2.5 Encashment: at exit, unused privilege leave up to 45 days is encashed at basic salary.

## 3. Family leave
3.1 Maternity leave: eligible women employees get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave, as per the Maternity Benefit Act.
3.2 Paternity leave: employees get 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.
3.3 Bereavement leave: employees get 3 days of paid leave on the death of an immediate family member.

## 4. Applying for leave
4.1 Leave must be applied for on the HRMS portal at least 3 working days in advance, except sick leave and bereavement leave.
4.2 Leave taken without approval is treated as loss of pay (LOP).
4.3 Employees also get 12 fixed holidays per year as per the holiday calendar.
