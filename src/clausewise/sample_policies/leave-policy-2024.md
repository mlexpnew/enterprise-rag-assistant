# Leave Policy (FY 2024-25)

## 1. Scope
1.1 This policy applies to all permanent employees of Nimbus Retail Pvt Ltd in India.

## 2. Leave entitlement
2.1 Privilege leave (PL): employees earn 18 days of privilege leave per calendar year, credited monthly at 1.5 days.
2.2 Casual leave (CL): employees get 8 days of casual leave per year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward.
2.3 Sick leave (SL): employees get 10 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for more than 2 consecutive days.
2.4 Carry forward: up to 30 days of unused privilege leave can be carried forward to the next year.

## 3. Applying for leave
3.1 Leave must be applied for on the HRMS portal at least 3 working days in advance, except sick leave.
3.2 Leave taken without approval is treated as loss of pay (LOP).
