# Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Hotels
1.1 Hotel limit per night in metro cities: ₹3,500 for grades G1 to G3 and ₹6,000 for grades G4 to G6.
1.2 Hotel limit per night in non-metro cities: ₹2,500 for grades G1 to G3 and ₹4,500 for grades G4 to G6.

## 2. Daily allowance
2.1 Per diem for food and local travel: ₹800 per day for grades G1 to G3 and ₹1,200 per day for grades G4 to G6.

## 3. Travel mode
3.1 Flights in economy class are allowed for journeys over 500 km; shorter journeys are by AC 2-tier train or company-approved cab.
3.2 Flight tickets must be booked through the company travel desk at least 7 days before travel.

## 4. Claims
4.1 Expense claims must be submitted on the HRMS portal within 30 days of returning from travel.
4.2 Receipts are mandatory for any single expense above ₹500.
