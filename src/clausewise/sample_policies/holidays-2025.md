# Holiday Calendar 2025

## 1. Holidays
1.1 Employees get 10 fixed holidays and 2 optional holidays in 2025.
1.2 Fixed holidays include Republic Day (26 January), Holi (14 March), Independence Day (15 August), Gandhi Jayanti (2 October), Diwali (20 October) and Christmas (25 December).
1.3 Optional holidays are chosen on the HRMS portal by 31 January.
